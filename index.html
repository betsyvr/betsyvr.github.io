Featured Projects

VisionDG: AI-assisted dangerous goods identification

Airline staff face challenges identifying dangerous goods in cargo. Designed an AI-assisted tool to streamline identification and decision-making.

Problem: Dangerous goods identification requires specialized knowledge and is prone to human error. Staff need faster, more reliable methods.

Solution: Created an AI-assisted identification tool with a user-friendly interface that guides staff through the decision-making process.

Outcome: Improved accuracy rates by 40% and reduced processing time by 50%.

Skills: AI/ML Integration, Safety-Critical Design, User Research, Prototyping

GetYourRefund: Human-Centered Tax Filing Support

A comprehensive tax filing platform designed with accessibility at its core. Simplified complex tax processes through intuitive design patterns.

Problem: Tax filing is complex and intimidating, especially for low-income filers who need help the most. Existing solutions were not accessible or affordable.

Solution: Designed an intuitive, accessible tax platform that breaks down complex processes into simple steps with clear guidance.

Outcome: Helped 10,000+ users successfully file taxes, with 95% satisfaction rating.

Skills: Accessibility Design, Service Design, Content Strategy, Usability Testing
